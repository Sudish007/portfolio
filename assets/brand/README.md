# sudish.dev identity

## Idea
The primary identity is **sudish.dev**. The compact mark is **`<s/>`**: a self-closing component whose content is Sudish's initial. It communicates coding immediately without adding terminals, circuits, gradients or decorative effects. The slash is the only colour accent and carries the idea of syntax, execution and closure.

## Logo system
- `mark.svg`: standard `<s/>` mark for profiles, app tiles, lockups and display at 48 px or larger.
- `favicon.svg`: optically enlarged and thickened variant for favicons and navigation at 16–32 px.
- `logo-horizontal-dark.svg`: primary lockup on dark backgrounds.
- `logo-horizontal-light.svg`: primary lockup on light backgrounds.
- `mark-mono.svg`: one-colour production mark.
- PNG exports: 32, 180, 192 and 512 px.

## Colours
- Ink: `#101218`
- Paper: `#f5f5f2`
- Accent slash: `#06b6d4` on the mark and dark contexts
- Light-context `.dev`: `#0e7490` (4.9:1 on paper)
- Slate: `#687080`

## Construction
- Brackets and slash use the same visual weight and square terminals.
- The lowercase `s` is an outlined Segoe UI Semibold glyph; the SVG has no runtime font dependency.
- The favicon is not a simple downscale: its brackets, slash and `s` are wider for optical clarity.

## Usage
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the slash width.
- Minimum digital size: favicon variant at 16 px; standard mark at 24 px.
- Keep the `sudish.dev` wordmark beside the optical small mark in navigation whenever space allows; use the small mark alone on phones, and the standard mark for avatars/large placements.
- Never add gradients, glow, drop shadows, circuits, nodes, rotations or additional punctuation.
- The slash is the only accent; brackets and `s` remain ink.
- For one-colour reproduction, use `mark-mono.svg`.
